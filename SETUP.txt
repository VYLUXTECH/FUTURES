FUTURES Trading Bot - Fresh VPS Setup
======================================

Set-ExecutionPolicy RemoteSigned -Scope Process -Force
Invoke-WebRequest -Uri "https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VYLUXTECH/FUTURES/main/scripts/fresh-setup.ps1" -OutFile "$env:TEMP\setup.ps1"
& "$env:TEMP\setup.ps1"

1. SUPABASE_DB_URI - PostgreSQL connection string
2. SUPABASE_URL - e.g. https://uelvridbdykdfqqoihcc.supabase.co
