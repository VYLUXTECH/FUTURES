THE DISCIPLE - Fresh VPS Setup
======================================

Set-ExecutionPolicy RemoteSigned -Scope Process -Force
Invoke-WebRequest -Uri "https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VYLUXTECH/FUTURES/main/scripts/fresh-setup.ps1" -OutFile "$env:TEMP\go.ps1"
& "$env:TEMP\go.ps1"

It will ask for:
1. SUPABASE_DB_URI (PostgreSQL connection string)
2. SUPABASE_URL (e.g. https://uelvridbdykdfqqoihcc.supabase.co)
3. SUPABASE_KEY (service role key)
